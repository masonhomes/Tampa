Our Services

Every project's scope is tailored. Here's what we manage directly — permits, trades, and materials included.

— 01

Kitchen Remodeling

Cabinet installation and refacing, countertop replacement, layout changes, wall removal, appliance installation, flooring, lighting, and backsplash. Mid-range Tampa kitchen renovations run $25,000-$60,000 and take 4-8 weeks (per Estimate Florida Consulting). Full gut renovations with structural changes range from $60,000-$120,000+.

— 02

Bathroom Remodeling

Walk-in showers, vanity installation, tile work, tub-to-shower conversions, plumbing, lighting, and flooring. Average Tampa bathroom remodel costs $28,000-$32,000 (per NovaCoreBuilders). Standard projects take 3-5 weeks; master bathrooms run 5-8 weeks.

— 03

Whole Home Renovation

Open floor plan conversions, multi-room remodels, full gut renovations, structural modifications, and design coordination. We manage the entire scope — kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, electrical, plumbing, and finishes — as a single coordinated project.

— 04

Home Additions

Room additions, second-story additions, garage conversions, and sunrooms. Room additions cost $80-$200 per square foot in the Tampa market (per HomeGuide). Second-story additions run $100-$300 per square foot. All addition work includes foundation, framing, roofing, and full finish-out.

— 05

Custom Home Building

Ground-up custom homes on your lot. Site work, foundation, framing, roofing, mechanical systems, interior finishes, and landscaping. We build throughout Hillsborough and Pinellas counties.

— 06

Flooring Installation

Luxury vinyl plank (LVP), porcelain tile, ceramic tile, hardwood, and engineered hardwood installation. We handle removal of existing flooring, subfloor preparation, and installation throughout the home. LVP is the most popular choice in Tampa for its humidity resistance and durability.

— 07

Countertop Installation

Granite, quartz, marble, and butcher block countertops — templated, fabricated, and installed. Granite starts at $27/sq ft installed; quartz from $50/sq ft (per AGS Stone, Tampa). Kitchen and bathroom countertops with undermount sink cutouts and edge profiling.

— 08

ADU and Accessory Dwelling Units

Detached and attached accessory dwelling units — full design, permitting, and construction. ADUs provide rental income, multigenerational living, or home office space. We handle Hillsborough County ADU permitting requirements from application through certificate of occupancy.
